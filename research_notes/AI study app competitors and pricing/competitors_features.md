# AI study-kit apps vs. Mnemo: features, pricing, platforms (observed 2026-10-14)

All prices USD unless noted. "Observed" = the date the page was fetched for these notes (2026-10-14) unless another date is given. Apple App Store prices are the US storefront's in-app-purchase (IAP) list; Apple does not label billing periods on these lists, so period attribution is noted where the listing itself gives it. Several official pricing pages (Quizlet, StudyFetch, Mindgrasp, Quizgecko, Knowunity, Thea+) block crawlers or omit prices, so App Store IAP lists are used as the primary price source there and third-party trackers are flagged as such.

## Key question 1: Thea (theastudy.com) — exact feature set, pricing, platforms, what changed when it started charging, and how much of Mnemo is a 1:1 match

### Takeaway
Thea (Theaai Corporation, Arlington VA) is a free-to-start iOS/Android/web app that turns uploads (90-100+ file types incl. PDF, DOCX, PPTX, images, MP3/MP4, YouTube) into Smart Study (adaptive Socratic questions), flashcards with spaced repetition, study guides, summaries, practice tests, games and podcasts; a paid "Thea+" tier ($12/mo or $48/yr on the US App Store) removes newly introduced daily question limits and ~2-hour wait times on Smart Study/practice tests, which is what users mean by Thea "starting to charge" (complaints cluster from early Oct 2026). Roughly half of Mnemo's feature list has a direct Thea counterpart; Thea has no confirmed concept map, exam-date planner, "explain it back", FSRS, BYOK, PDF/Markdown export of guides beyond "download", or E2E/no-account sync.

### Inferences
- Thea+ launched between late April 2026 ("pricing not finalized") and early October 2026 (first paywall complaints dated Oct 3); the US price is $12/mo or $48/yr (=$4/mo effective). The "charging" change is a metering change (daily caps + ~2 h cooldown on Smart Study and ~15-question cap on tests), not removal of free kit creation.
- Mnemo vs Thea 1:1 match (based on confirmed Thea features): MATCH — multi-format upload incl. photos/audio/video/YouTube; adaptive Smart Study MCQ (+ short answer) targeting weak spots; AI-graded short answers (Thea offers "short answer" question type); spaced-repetition flashcards; practice tests; study guides/summaries (downloadable); AI podcast; mini games; Socratic questioning (as question mode, not a free-form tutor chat); mastery tracking; 60+ languages (Thea 80+); cross-device sync (account-based); PWA/web app. PARTIAL — hints and true/false not confirmed; export formats not specified (only "download"). NO MATCH / NOT CONFIRMED — concept map; FSRS specifically; AI flashcard top-ups; "Explain it back"; free-form Socratic tutor chat; exam-date planner with quota and forgetting-curve forecast; streaks (Daily Challenges exist as a post title but content not retrievable); E2E-encrypted, account-less sync code; BYOK/Gemini key; user-side API cost model.
- Approximately 11-12 of Mnemo's ~20 listed features have a confirmed Thea equivalent.

### Gaps
- Exact free daily question cap and cooldown are not published; only user reports (2 h wait, ~15 test questions) exist.
- Exact Thea+ launch date not found; Google Play rating (4.7 per Thea's own site) could not be independently verified (Play Store and AppBrain pages returned 404/403).
- Whether Thea's podcast is two-host; whether Thea has a free-form chat tutor or concept map; underlying AI model — none published.

## Key question 2: Feature set, price and platforms for each competitor

### Takeaway
The market splits into (a) upload-to-study-kit generalists (Knowt, StudyFetch, Turbo AI, Mindgrasp, Thea, Coursology, Quizgecko, Revisely, Gizmo) typically priced $8-25/mo monthly or $48-150/yr with metered free tiers; (b) incumbents (Quizlet, NotebookLM/Gemini Notebook, Knowunity) with large user bases; and (c) spaced-repetition-first tools (Anki, RemNote, Mochi, Brainscape) with the strongest scheduling (FSRS in Anki/RemNote/Mochi) but weaker upload-to-kit pipelines. All run their own AI backend; none offers BYOK. Monic AI shut down 2026-03-07; Edexia is a teacher grading tool, not a study app.

## Key question 4: Which Mnemo features are unusual or unique, and which are table stakes?

### Takeaway
Table stakes in Oct 2026: multi-format upload (PDF/slides/YouTube/audio/photos), AI flashcards, MCQ quizzes/practice tests, summaries/study guides, spaced repetition of some kind, an AI chat/tutor, progress tracking and streaks, an AI podcast, and iOS+Android+web. Unusual: concept map, FSRS, AI-graded short answers inside an adaptive loop, exam-date planner with forgetting-curve forecast, explain-it-back with coverage check, PDF/Markdown export. Unique (no competitor found): bring-your-own Gemini key with user-paid API cost, account-less E2E-encrypted sync via sync code, and the combination of FSRS + exam planner + kit generation in one free PWA.

### Cited Findings
- Table stakes evidence: upload-to-flashcards/quizzes/notes/podcast in Knowt, Turbo, StudyFetch, Thea, Mindgrasp — see Key question 2 sources ([knowt.com/plans](https://knowt.com/plans), [turbo.ai](https://www.turbo.ai/), [StudyFetch App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/studyfetch-make-learning-easy/id6663574866), [Mindgrasp App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mindgrasp/id1638182014)); streaks/achievements in StudyFetch — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/studyfetch-make-learning-easy/id6663574866); mastery tracking in Thea — [features](https://www.theastudy.com/features); 70-80+ languages in Thea and NotebookLM video overviews — [Thea App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/thea-study-smart/id6742800289), [NotebookLM App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/google-notebooklm/id6737527615).
- Concept/mind map: NotebookLM has a Mind Map (per third-party guides) but not in the fetched App Store description; no kit generator in this set lists one. — [dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/best-ai-study-tools)
- FSRS only in Anki/RemNote/Mochi/StudyLess (Key question 3 sources).
- Exam-date planning only RemNote/StudyLess/CogniCards (Key question 3 sources).
- Export to PDF/Markdown: Thea offers "downloadable study guides" (format unspecified) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/thea-study-smart/id6742800289); Mochi and Monic offered import/export (Anki) — [mochi.cards/pricing](https://mochi.cards/pricing). Not a common marketed feature elsewhere.
- BYOK, E2EE, no account: none found (Key question 3).
- Reviewers' common recommendation is to combine NotebookLM (reading/podcast) with Knowt or Anki (recall) because no single tool does both well. — [dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/best-ai-study-tools), [studr](https://studr.app/blog/notebooklm-alternative-for-students/)

### Inferences
- Mnemo's cost positioning (user pays ~$1.50-9/mo of Gemini API or $0 on free tier) undercuts every competitor's paid tier ($5-25/mo) and is the only model where question volume is not metered by the vendor — directly addressing the exact complaint driving Thea's recent 1-2 star reviews ("wait 2h for questions").
- The downside is table-stakes friction: every competitor is zero-setup; Mnemo requires obtaining a Gemini API key, which no mainstream consumer study app asks of users.

### Gaps
- No usage or market-share data on how many students would tolerate a BYOK setup; no survey evidence found.
